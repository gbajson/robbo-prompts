domains: facebook.com, fb.com
- A post has a small "X" button in its top right corner (OCR may list it as "x", "X" or a multiplication sign). It hides the post from the feed; it does not delete it. The "..." button next to it opens the post's menu.
- Do not click "Like", "Share" or "Send" unless the goal says so: they act at once and cannot be undone by closing the page.
- A login wall or a "See more on Facebook" dialog may cover the page: do not log in unless the goal says so; ask for the values with need.
- The top right has the icons Menu (a grid of dots: Create and the app's links, no "Log out"), Messenger, Notifications and, last, the profile photo (its name may be "Your profile" or "Account"). Log out is in the menu of the photo ("Log Out" or "Wyloguj się"). The photo is a picture, not an icon: it may be missing from the list; the DOM's element is added after the others.
- To read the comments of a post in the search results or the feed, click the post's own button "Comment on <name>'s post, N comments" (next to Like and Share, under the post). The name of the page or person at the top of the post is a link to their profile, not to the post; a link with only a number (shares, reactions) does not open the comments.
- The comments of a post open in a dialog over the search results, with a "Close" button at its top right. At first the dialog shows only the post and the box "Comment as ...": the comments are not loaded yet, and that does not mean there are none (the post's button says how many). To load them: click the post's comment button, keep the mouse over the post in the dialog (move it there, or click on the post's plain text, not on a link), scroll down, click "Most relevant" above the comments and choose "All comments". Scroll again until the comments appear; the page's "scrolling changed nothing" warning does not mean there are no comments, only that the mouse was not over the post.
- The dialog holds only that post's comments. When, after "All comments", scrolling shows nothing new (the same names and texts again), you have read all of them; click "Close" and go on to the next post in the results. Do not scroll the results behind the dialog while it is open.
- "View N replies" under a comment opens its replies; The link of one comment is its time ("2h", or a date) next to the author's name: click it to open the comment on its own and read its address.
