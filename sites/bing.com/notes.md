domains: bing.com
- To ask Bing's AI (Copilot Search) without an account: type the question into the search box of bing.com and press Return, then click the "AI Mode" tab above the results; the answer is written there, wait until it stops growing. copilot.microsoft.com asks to sign in: do not use it.
- The "AI" button next to the search box only opens a menu of image and text tools, not the chat.
- In AI Mode the text of the answer is often not listed (only its citation buttons and "<n> Sources"), and copy_page gives almost nothing: select from the question (the text at the top) to the "Sources" link under the answer, copy_selection it with a name, and read it.
