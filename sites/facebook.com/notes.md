domains: facebook.com, fb.com
- The Feed ("news feed", "Aktualności"; the Home page facebook.com/, the house icon) shows the posts of friends and pages. A profile's timeline ("wall", "tablica", "oś czasu") shows that person's posts. A "wall" with no person named is the Feed. The first post is the top one not marked "Sponsored".
- A post has a small "X" button in its top right corner (OCR: "x", "X" or "×"). It hides the post from the feed; it does not delete it. The "..." button next to it opens the post's menu.
- Do not click "Like", "Share" or "Send" unless the goal says so: they act at once and cannot be undone by closing the page.
- A login wall or a "See more on Facebook" dialog may cover the page: do not log in unless the goal says so; ask for the values with need.
- The top right has the icons Menu (a grid of dots: Create and the app's links, no "Log out"), Messenger, Notifications and, last, the profile photo (its name may be "Your profile" or "Account"). Log out is in the menu of the photo ("Log Out" or "Wyloguj się"). The photo may be missing from the list (its DOM element comes last).
- To read the comments of a post in the search results or the feed, click the post's own button "Comment on <name>'s post, N comments" (next to Like and Share, under the post). The name of the page or person at the top of the post is a link to their profile, not to the post; a link with only a number (shares, reactions) does not open the comments.
- The comments of a post open in a dialog over the search results, with a "Close" button at its top right. At first the dialog shows only the post and the box "Comment as ...": the comments are not loaded yet, and that does not mean there are none (the post's button says how many). To load them: click the post's comment button, keep the mouse over the post in the dialog (move it there, or click on the post's plain text, not on a link), scroll down, click "Most relevant" above the comments and choose the order the goal asks for ("Newest", "All comments"; "All comments" when it says none). After a choice in that menu the mouse is outside the dialog: click on plain text inside the dialog again, then scroll until the comments appear; the page's "scrolling changed nothing" warning does not mean there are no comments, only that the mouse was not over the post.
- The dialog holds only that post's comments. When, after "All comments", scrolling shows nothing new (the same names and texts again), you have read all of them; click "Close" and go on to the next post in the results. Do not scroll the results behind the dialog while it is open.
- The link of a post is its time ("2h", "12 min", a date) next to the author's name. The page hides that text and its address (random letters, `facebook.com/?__cft__...`), so never answer with them, and "Copy link" in the "..." menu only fills the clipboard. Click the time: the post opens on its own page, and its "Page address now" is the link to answer with, whole.
- "View N replies" under a comment opens its replies: open them only when the goal asks for replies. The link of one comment is its time ("2h", or a date) next to the author's name: click it to open the comment on its own and read its address.
- To list or read many comments, do not note them one by one from the screen: scroll the dialog until as many comments as the goal wants are loaded, then copy_page and read it (for: "the comments in order: author and text").
